# Outbound replies — six of the 500

`leads/funnel-12mo.csv` says **500 people were labelled `interested`** over twelve months. It also
says that label came from an LLM classifier and **not from a human**.

145 of those 500 reached the CRM as an opportunity — 29%, which session 1's audit flagged as the
first of two anomalies in this funnel.

These are six replies the classifier labelled `interested`. They are here so that you can do, on
six rows, the thing nobody at ServiceGrid has ever done on five hundred: **read them and decide
whether the label was right.**

⚠️ Six rows cannot measure a rate. A 29% population rate is entirely consistent with a small
sample landing anywhere between one and four. Do not report a percentage off this file — the point
is the *kind* of error, not its size.
