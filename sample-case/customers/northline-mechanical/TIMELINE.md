## 2026-03-12 · call 1 of 10 · 24 min · Mike R. (rep) · Lisa Grant (CFO & co-owner)
**What they asked for:** a straight answer on QuickBooks Enterprise — *"Do your reports integrate
with QuickBooks Enterprise? That's what accounting runs on."* Stated as pass/fail in the next
breath: *"that's really the dealbreaker for us. If it doesn't talk to QuickBooks Enterprise, it's a
non-starter."* Also: a case study from a company similar to Northline.
**What was promised:** the QuickBooks answer ("let me confirm"), and a package — case studies,
product overview, pricing guide.
**What was actually delivered by the next call:** the package, same day. **Not the QuickBooks
answer.** The case study was Kessler, a plumbing company in Ohio — Northline is HVAC/mechanical at
~80 techs.
**Open after this call:** the dealbreaker. Also her stated need, twice cut off mid-sentence:
*"I was mainly curious about—"* and *"The board wants more granular job-level profitability and—"*

## 2026-03-17 · call 4 of 10 · 8 min · Mike · Lisa
